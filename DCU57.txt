#  how do you view or clear what you've blocked?


export your settings
dcu-cli.exe /configure -exportSettings=C:\Temp

this will create a .xml file.  Open that file and search for BLOCK. 

Property Name="LocalBlockedSWB">
<ValueList>
<Value>2vdpw</Value>
</ValueList>
</Property>


you should see your value


# to clear the value

dcu-cli /configure -clearlocalblockedswb
